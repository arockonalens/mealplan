01

Incline Chest Press

Technogym Selection Pro · incline chest press

Technogym Chest Press — Selection 700 or 900

Seated, upright back pad, two independent lever arms with handles beside your chest, weight stack behind you. Most Technogym floors have no dedicated incline machine: use the standard chest press with the seat dropped to its lowest setting, which raises the handles relative to your chest and gives you the angle. Failing that, an adjustable bench at 30° with dumbbells in the Pure area.

4sets8–10reps90srest2-1-1tempo
  • Set the seat so the handles sit at upper-chest height, not shoulder height. Too high and it becomes a shoulder press.
  • Press up and slightly in. Stop just short of locking the elbows — the tension is the point, not the lockout.
  • Upper chest is the part almost everyone under-builds, which is why it goes first while you are fresh.
  • Last two reps should be genuinely hard with form intact. If rep 10 is easy, the weight goes up next session.

02

Chest Press

Technogym Selection Pro · chest press

Technogym Chest Press — the same machine as 01

Raise the seat so the handles line up with the middle of your chest. Same machine, one pin change, completely different emphasis. Do not go and queue for a second one.

3sets8–10reps90srest2-1-1tempo
  • Shoulder blades pulled back and down into the pad, and keep them there the whole set. Letting them roll forward moves the work to the front delts.
  • Lower for a slow count of two. The stretched part is where most of the growth comes from and it is the part people rush.
  • Feet flat, no arching off the seat to move more weight.

03

Pectoral Machine

Technogym Selection Pro · pectoral machine (pec deck)

Technogym Pectoral Machine — often a combined Pectoral / Rear Delt

The giveaway is the arms: vertical pads you press with your forearms, not handles you push with your hands. If it has handles pointing forward you are at the chest press. If it is the combined pec/rear-delt version, the arms have to be swung round to the front position before you sit down.

3sets12–15reps60srest2-1-2tempo
  • A wide arc, elbows soft and fixed at that angle — if the elbow angle changes you are pressing, not flying.
  • Squeeze for a full second where the pads meet. This is the only point in the session where the chest is fully shortened.
  • Lighter than feels impressive. This is a stretch-and-squeeze exercise, not a load exercise.

04

Cable Crossover, low to high

Technogym Pure · dual adjustable pulley, both arms set low

Technogym Dual Adjustable Pulley, cable station, or Kinesis

Two tall uprights with a carriage that slides up and down and a D-handle on each cable. Slide both carriages to the bottom. If your gym has the wall-mounted Kinesis, that works too and the cables move more freely.

3sets12–15reps60srest2-1-2tempo
  • Pulleys at the bottom setting. Sweep up and in, hands finishing around collarbone height.
  • Step one foot forward and lean in slightly. Standing straight up lets the cables pull you backwards.
  • The cable keeps tension on at the top where a machine loses it — that is the whole reason this is here and not a second press.

05

Assisted Dip

Technogym Selection Pro · assisted chin/dip

Technogym Selection Assisted Chin / Dip

Tall frame, pull-up bar on top, dip bars at waist height, and a padded platform for your knees or feet. The stack takes weight off you rather than adding it, so a higher number is more help, not less — that is backwards from every other machine here and it catches everyone out once.

3sets8–12reps60srest2-0-1tempo
  • Lean forward and let the chest lead. Staying bolt upright turns this into a triceps exercise — the forward lean is the entire difference.
  • Set the assist so you reach 8–12 with the last two genuinely hard. Reduce the assistance as the weeks go, that is the progression.
  • Stop when the upper arms reach parallel. Going deeper looks impressive and is where shoulders get hurt.
  • Lower chest gets hit here and nowhere else in this session, which is why it earns a slot.

Scan the QR on the machine console with the Technogym app for their own video of the movement.

On frequency. One chest session a week will build some muscle, but the same 16 sets split across two sessions builds noticeably more — muscle protein synthesis runs about 48 hours, so twice a week catches a second wave. With four training days, chest on day one and day three with this session split in half would beat doing it all at once. Your call; this is the 45 minutes you asked for.
Load and progression. Pick a weight where the last two reps are hard but your form holds. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add the smallest increment next session. That is the whole method — there is nothing cleverer, it just has to happen every week for months.

01

Triceps Pushdown

Rope on the high pulley

Technogym Dual Adjustable Pulley · cable station. Rope attachment, carriage at the top. Elbows pinned to your ribs and kept there — the second they drift forward it becomes a shoulder movement.

4sets10–12reps60srest2-1-1tempo
  • Split the rope apart at the bottom and turn the knuckles slightly out. That last inch is where the long head actually works.
  • Triceps are two thirds of your arm. They get the volume and they get you while you are fresh.
  • Lean in a few degrees, but do not turn it into a standing press by rocking.

02

Standing Cable Curl

Straight or EZ bar on the low pulley

Technogym Dual Adjustable Pulley · cable station. Carriage all the way down, bar attachment. Stand a pace back from the column so there is tension on the cable before you even start.

4sets10–12reps60srest2-1-2tempo
  • This is the reason to curl on a cable rather than with dumbbells: the load never drops off at the top. A dumbbell goes weightless at the peak; the cable does not.
  • Elbows stay at your sides and still. If they swing forward, the front delts are taking it.
  • Stop just short of straight at the bottom and keep the tension on.

03

Overhead Cable Extension

Rope on the low pulley

Technogym Dual Adjustable Pulley · cable station. Face away from the machine, rope over your shoulders, elbows pointing forward at the ceiling.

3sets12–15reps60srest2-1-2tempo
  • Overhead is the only position that puts the long head of the triceps under a full stretch, which is why this is not just a repeat of the pushdown.
  • Elbows stay pointing forward and still. Only the forearms move.
  • Lighter than the pushdown, and lean forward slightly so the cable does not pull you backwards.

04

Cable Hammer Curl

Rope on the low pulley

Technogym Dual Adjustable Pulley · cable station. Rope attachment, neutral grip, thumbs up the whole way and never rotating.

3sets10–12reps60srest2-0-1tempo
  • The neutral grip hits the brachialis, which sits underneath the bicep and pushes it up. It is the muscle that makes arms look thick from the side.
  • Pull the rope ends slightly apart at the top rather than letting them clash together.
  • No swinging. If your back is moving, drop a plate.

05

Reverse Grip Pushdown

Straight bar on the high pulley

Technogym Dual Adjustable Pulley · cable station. Same setup as 01 with an underhand grip on a straight bar. Two sets to finish, nothing heavy.

  • Much lighter than the rope pushdown. The grip is weaker and the point is the angle, not the load.
  • Wrists straight, elbows locked to the sides, and squeeze at the bottom.

Two of these are loaded and that is deliberate. Abs are muscle and they grow the same way everything else does — by getting harder over time. 01

Hanging Leg Raise

Technogym Selection Assisted Chin / Dip, or a captain’s chair

Use the dip bars with your back against the pad, or hang from the bar above if your grip holds.

3sets10–15reps60srest2-1-2tempo
  • Curl the pelvis up towards your ribs. Lifting the legs with a flat back is a hip flexor exercise and most people do it for years without realising.
  • Knees bent is the honest version if straight legs make your back arch. Progress to straight over time.
  • Slow on the way down. Dropping the legs wastes the half that counts.

02

Cable Crunch

Technogym cable station, rope on the high pulley

Kneel facing the machine, rope beside your ears, hips fixed.

3sets12–15reps60srest2-1-2tempo
  • This is the only ab exercise here you can add weight to, which makes it the one that actually grows the muscle. Abs respond to progressive overload like anything else.
  • Crunch with the spine, not the hips. If your bum is moving back towards your heels you are doing a hip hinge.
  • Round down through the upper back and stop when the stretch runs out.

03

Abdominal Crunch

Technogym Selection Abdominal Crunch

Seated, chest pad across the front, feet hooked. Loaded from a stack so you can measure progress.

3sets15–20reps45srest2-1-2tempo
  • Push through the chest pad, not the handles. Pulling with the arms is the standard way to make this machine do nothing.
  • Short range, controlled. This is not a sit-up and you are not trying to fold in half.
  • Higher reps here than the cable crunch — different stimulus, and it is the safer one to chase a burn on.

Standing Cable Wood Chop

Technogym Dual Adjustable Pulley, handle set high

Pull down and across the body, rotating through the ribs and letting the back foot pivot.

  • Arms stay fairly straight — this is a torso movement, not a lat pulldown with a twist.
  • Let the back heel come off the floor. Fighting the rotation at the hip is how backs get tweaked.

05

Pallof Press

Technogym Dual Adjustable Pulley · handle at chest height

Stand side-on to the column, feet shoulder width, handle clasped in both hands at the sternum. Step away until the cable is properly loaded.

3sets10–12 each sidereps45srest2-1-2tempo
  • The cable is trying to twist you towards the machine. The entire exercise is refusing to let it. Nothing about your torso should move.
  • Press straight out and back in. The further the handle gets from your chest, the harder the cable pulls — that is the rep.
  • Same job as a plank, bracing the core against a force, except you can add weight to it. A plank you can only make longer, which stops being training and starts being time.
  • Ribs down, glutes tight, breathe out as you press. If your shoulders rotate at all, drop a plate.

Freezer plan

One shop, seven days — nothing sits too long

Raw chicken should not be sitting in the fridge from Sunday to Wednesday, and cooked meat is a three-day proposition. This is what moves where, and when.

  1. Sunday, unpacking the bags

    Chicken breast straight to the freezer — it is not needed until Wednesday. Thigh fillets and beef chuck to the fridge; both get cooked within 24 hours.

  2. Sunday, after cooking

    Half the focaccia sliced and frozen. Half the pulled thigh into the freezer for Wednesday’s melt; the rest stays in the fridge for Tuesday.

  3. Sunday night, after dinner

    Friday’s half of the braise and its strained liquid straight into the freezer. Left in the fridge it would be five days old by Friday.

  4. Tuesday night

    Wednesday’s pulled thigh out of the freezer into the fridge.

  5. Wednesday, while the slow cooker runs

    Bake the frittata with whatever the fridge has accumulated plus the ham. Cool, cut into six, fridge. That is the savoury snack from Thursday to Saturday.

  6. Wednesday night

    Portion Saturday’s share of the cooked breast into the freezer. Left in the fridge it would be four days old by Saturday, which is past where I would eat it.

  7. Thursday night

    The braise out of the freezer into the fridge for Friday’s ragu, and the last of the focaccia with it.

The rule underneath all of this: cooked dishes get eaten within about three days or they get frozen, and anything frozen thaws in the fridge overnight rather than on the bench. For actual times and temperatures, Food Standards Australia New Zealand rather than a meal plan.
