Sunday, in order

≈2 hr elapsed · ~45 min hands-on

The sequence matters. The dough proves while everything else cooks, the chickpeas need a head start, and the oven runs three jobs back to back — chicken, then slice, then focaccia, each hotter than the last.

  1. Mix the focaccia dough — first, before anything

    It needs 2–3 hours to prove and it does that on its own while the rest of Sunday happens. Ten minutes of actual work.

  2. Quick-soak the chickpeas

    Cover 500 g dried chickpeas with water, boil 5 minutes, off the heat, stand 1 hour.

  3. Oven to 200 °C — roast the thighs

    All 1 kg, tossed in oil, paprika, cumin, salt. 25–30 min. Sunday dinner plus two lunches.

  4. While the oven runs — build the dahl

    500 g red lentils, 2 diced onions, 3 grated carrots, 1 can tomatoes, curry powder, 1.5 L water. Simmer 30 min.

  5. Cook 500 g rice, shred the slaw

    Rice spread on a tray to cool fast. Half a cabbage and 2 grated carrots, stored dry and undressed.

  6. Chicken out — oven down to 180 °C — bake the oat slice

    25–30 min. Cool it completely in the tin before cutting or it crumbles.

  7. Oven up to 220 °C — dimple and bake the focaccia

    20–25 min until deep gold. Last thing in, because it wants the hottest oven.

  8. Drain the chickpeas, portion everything

    Chickpeas to the fridge for bowls and the frittata. Slice and freeze half the focaccia now — it is excellent on day one and tired by day four.

Skipping the soak? Two 400 g cans replace the dried chickpeas. Costs about $1.50 more and drops roughly 60 g of protein off the week.

  • Three days, then freeze. This plan assumes cooked dishes get eaten within about three days. That's deliberately conservative — portion and freeze anything past it rather than pushing your luck.
  • Cool fast, then fridge. Shallow containers, spread out. A full hot pot straight in warms everything around it.
  • Rice needs care. Cool it quickly, refrigerate promptly, reheat once only until steaming. That's why Saturday's fried rice wants properly chilled rice.
  • For actual internal temperatures and fridge and freezer times in Australia, check Food Standards Australia New Zealand or your state health authority — not a meal plan.
