Everything gets trained twice a week — once on its own day and again in Saturday’s upper session. That is the point of the split: muscle protein synthesis runs about 48 hours, so twice catches a second wave that once does not. Friday and Sunday are the rest days, and they are real ones, not optional. Monday to Thursday runs four straight, which is fine because no two consecutive days train the same muscle as the primary. Abs land Tuesday and Thursday. The three machines you actually live on — Vertical Traction (their pulldown), Low Row and the Abdominal Crunch — are the spine of Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, so the week is built around what you are already using rather than around what a programme thinks you should.

01

Incline Chest Press

Technogym Selection Pro · incline chest press

Technogym Chest Press — Selection 700 or 900

Seated, upright back pad, two independent lever arms with handles beside your chest, weight stack behind you. Most Technogym floors have no dedicated incline machine: use the standard chest press with the seat dropped to its lowest setting, which raises the handles relative to your chest and gives you the angle. Failing that, an adjustable bench at 30° with dumbbells in the Pure area.

4sets8–10reps90srest2-1-1tempo
  • Set the seat so the handles sit at upper-chest height, not shoulder height. Too high and it becomes a shoulder press.
  • Press up and slightly in. Stop just short of locking the elbows — the tension is the point, not the lockout.
  • Upper chest is the part almost everyone under-builds, which is why it goes first while you are fresh.
  • Last two reps should be genuinely hard with form intact. If rep 10 is easy, the weight goes up next session.

02

Chest Press

Technogym Selection Pro · chest press

Technogym Chest Press — the same machine as 01

Raise the seat so the handles line up with the middle of your chest. Same machine, one pin change, completely different emphasis. Do not go and queue for a second one.

3sets8–10reps90srest2-1-1tempo
  • Shoulder blades pulled back and down into the pad, and keep them there the whole set. Letting them roll forward moves the work to the front delts.
  • Lower for a slow count of two. The stretched part is where most of the growth comes from and it is the part people rush.
  • Feet flat, no arching off the seat to move more weight.

03

Pectoral Machine

Technogym Selection Pro · pectoral machine (pec deck)

Technogym Pectoral Machine — often a combined Pectoral / Rear Delt

The giveaway is the arms: vertical pads you press with your forearms, not handles you push with your hands. If it has handles pointing forward you are at the chest press. If it is the combined pec/rear-delt version, the arms have to be swung round to the front position before you sit down.

3sets12–15reps60srest2-1-2tempo
  • A wide arc, elbows soft and fixed at that angle — if the elbow angle changes you are pressing, not flying.
  • Squeeze for a full second where the pads meet. This is the only point in the session where the chest is fully shortened.
  • Lighter than feels impressive. This is a stretch-and-squeeze exercise, not a load exercise.

Cable Crossover, low to high

Technogym Dual Adjustable Pulley, cable station, or Kinesis

Two tall uprights with a carriage that slides up and down and a D-handle on each cable. Slide both carriages to the bottom. If your gym has the wall-mounted Kinesis, that works too and the cables move more freely.

05

Assisted Dip

Technogym Selection Pro · assisted chin/dip

Technogym Selection Assisted Chin / Dip

Tall frame, pull-up bar on top, dip bars at waist height, and a padded platform for your knees or feet. The stack takes weight off you rather than adding it, so a higher number is more help, not less — that is backwards from every other machine here and it catches everyone out once.

3sets8–12reps60srest2-0-1tempo
  • Lean forward and let the chest lead. Staying bolt upright turns this into a triceps exercise — the forward lean is the entire difference.
  • Set the assist so you reach 8–12 with the last two genuinely hard. Reduce the assistance as the weeks go, that is the progression.
  • Stop when the upper arms reach parallel. Going deeper looks impressive and is where shoulders get hurt.
  • Lower chest gets hit here and nowhere else in this session, which is why it earns a slot.

01

Triceps Pushdown

Rope on the high pulley

Technogym Dual Adjustable Pulley · cable station. Rope attachment, carriage at the top. Elbows pinned to your ribs and kept there — the second they drift forward it becomes a shoulder movement.

4sets10–12reps60srest2-1-1tempo
  • Split the rope apart at the bottom and turn the knuckles slightly out. That last inch is where the long head actually works.
  • Triceps are two thirds of your arm. They get the volume and they get you while you are fresh.
  • Lean in a few degrees, but do not turn it into a standing press by rocking.

Standing Cable Curl

  • Stop just short of straight at the bottom and keep the tension on.

Abdominal Crunch

Technogym Selection Abdominal Crunch

Seated, chest pad across the front, feet hooked. Loaded from a stack so you can measure progress.

Kneel facing the machine, rope beside your ears, hips fixed.

05

Pallof Press

Technogym Dual Adjustable Pulley · handle at chest height

Stand side-on to the column, feet shoulder width, handle clasped in both hands at the sternum. Step away until the cable is properly loaded.

3sets10–12 each sidereps45srest2-1-2tempo
  • The cable is trying to twist you towards the machine. The entire exercise is refusing to let it. Nothing about your torso should move.
  • Press straight out and back in. The further the handle gets from your chest, the harder the cable pulls — that is the rep.
  • Same job as a plank, bracing the core against a force, except you can add weight to it. A plank you can only make longer, which stops being training and starts being time.
  • Ribs down, glutes tight, breathe out as you press. If your shoulders rotate at all, drop a plate.

Sunday cook-up

Four batches · the whole week in one afternoon

This is a bigger Sunday than usual — four batch dishes plus two bakes — but it buys you a week with almost no weekday cooking. Order matters more than it has before, because the oven has to go up and down and two things need soaking first.

  1. Dough and both soaks, before anything else

    Focaccia dough needs three hours. Put the oil in it this time. Then quick-soak the chickpeas and the cannellini in separate pots: cover, hard boil five minutes, off the heat, stand an hour.

  2. Banana bread — first into the oven, and it is the long one

    Four bananas mashed, fifth split for the top. No butter and no oil — yoghurt and banana do that job. 170 °C for a full hour, foil over at forty minutes if it browns early. Five bananas is a lot of moisture and a hot oven sets the outside while the middle stays raw.

  3. Burrito potatoes can start while the cookies bake

    Diced small, in oil, in a wide pan on the cooktop. Fifteen minutes to properly crisp. The oven is busy and this is the long cooktop job, so they overlap neatly.

  4. Legumes on to simmer, and fold the dough at 45 minutes

    Both pots 45–50 minutes until tender but holding shape. Keep a cup of the bean water — it is what stops the Tuscan sauce being a thin cream slick. Wet hand on the dough, not floured.

  5. Burrito filling — potatoes first, and brown them hard

    Diced small, fifteen minutes, properly crisp at the edges. Then onion, chiles, spice. Scramble the eggs soft and underdone; they cook again on reheating. Then let it all go completely cold before you roll anything — warm filling steams the wrap and you get eight soggy burritos.

  6. Tuscan chicken — the kilo of thigh

    Browned in batches, never crowded. Onion, garlic, sun-dried tomatoes in the chicken fat, cream, reduce, then beans and their water. Spinach off the heat at the very end. Cooked in, it goes khaki and waters down everything you just reduced.

  7. Peanut bowls — and the dressing goes in its own jar

    Sear the 750 g of thigh, cook the rice and spread it on a tray to cool fast. Assemble the six bowls dry: rice, chicken, raw cabbage and carrot. Nothing gets dressed. Dressed slaw is limp inside a day and that is the whole reason these keep four days.

  8. Oven up to 220 °C — focaccia last

    Heaps of oil in the tray. Oiled fingers, press through to the base. Rotate the topping this week — fennel seed and sea salt, or za’atar. Twenty-two minutes.

  9. Whipped feta, two minutes

    100 g feta, 100 g yoghurt, lemon, pepper, oil, blitzed until completely smooth. If it is still grainy it eats like crumbled cheese and the point is lost.

The oven only goes up now: 170 for the banana bread, then 220 for the focaccia. Dropping the pumpkin loaf took a whole temperature step out of the afternoon, and about an hour of oven time with it. Everything else is cooktop and happens around the dough proving.

What moves where

Twenty-six portions, seven days

Almost all of this is cooked today, so the real work is portioning it correctly before you sit down. Four days is the limit on cooked egg and cooked chicken; anything later than Wednesday goes in the freezer tonight.

  1. Unpacking — one job

    Split the 2 kg of thigh three ways: 1 kg out for the Tuscan, 750 g out for the bowls, and 250 g straight to the freezer for Wednesday’s quesadillas. Three days raw in the fridge is the limit and Wednesday is past it.

  2. Burritos — four and four

    Wrapped individually. Four to the fridge for Sunday, Tuesday; four to the freezer for Thursday and Saturday. From the fridge they are 8 minutes in the air fryer; from frozen, 25 at 170 °C.

  3. Tuscan — two, two and two

    Two eaten tonight, two to the fridge for Monday, two to the freezer for Friday. Six days in the fridge would be well past it.

  4. Bowls — six dry, one jar

    All six assembled and lidded, dressing separate. They are Monday, Wednesday and Friday lunches.

  5. Bakes

    Focaccia cut into 12, half fridge half frozen. Banana bread cools completely before it is cut — a fat-free loaf cut hot tears and goes gummy. Ten slices is one each a day for five days, and it stales faster than a buttered loaf, so slice and freeze from Wednesday and toast it from frozen.

  6. Thursday night

    Wednesday’s quesadilla chicken is already eaten by then — nothing to move. Take the last focaccia out of the freezer for Friday’s Tuscan.

  7. Saturday — the gap

    Twenty-six batch portions against twenty-eight slots. Saturday’s dinner is deliberately undefined: whatever is left, fried rice. If the fridge has given you nothing with protein in it, add two eggs or a tin of beans rather than eating a bowl of rice.

The rule underneath all of this: cooked dishes get eaten within about three days or they get frozen, and anything frozen thaws in the fridge overnight rather than on the bench. For actual times and temperatures, Food Standards Australia New Zealand rather than a meal plan.
